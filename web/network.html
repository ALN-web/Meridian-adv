Every truck out of Pune to Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Delhi and Ahmedabad is billed by one carrier, through one portal built in 2009. There's no API and no export. Until now, two people spent three days a month clicking through it.

02 · Portal

A portal that
fights back.

The portal is slow, throws error pages, shows pop-ups that block clicks, logs you out mid-task and asks for one-time passwords. The agent retries with backoff, hands every OTP and CAPTCHA to a person, and if it's logged out, resumes at the next invoice instead of starting over.

03 · Rules

Eight rules.
Zero guesses.

Every line is checked in code: the weight within 2%, the rate on the booking date, the month's fuel surcharge, detention against the delivery proof, duplicates, unknown shipments, missing proofs and the GST total. Amounts compare to within ₹1. The model never does the maths.

04 · Dispute

Disputes,
only when right.

Only an overcharge above ₹500 becomes a dispute, and only after a person approves it. The agent checks the portal's dispute list before submitting and again after. A success message is not proof, and nothing is ever raised twice.

05 · Record

Every step,
on the record.

One trace per task with one span per step: the page, the action, the outcome, retries and time. Every write gets a masked screenshot, and an evaluation page scores each run against the answer key. No passwords, names or phone numbers ever leave the masks.
