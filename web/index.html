About Meridian

Built on one question: did the carrier bill us for what it actually delivered? TransIndus sends around 600 invoices a month through a portal with no API. Our agent reads every one.

Two people used to spend three days a month clicking through the carrier portal: opening invoices, downloading delivery proofs, checking weights and rates, and raising disputes for overcharges.

Now the agent does the clicking and the checking. The rules run in code, a person approves every dispute, and every step leaves a trace.

How it works

A portal that fights back. An agent that doesn't give up.

01

Operate

Logs in, hands every one-time password and CAPTCHA to a person, and rides out slow pages, error pages, pop-ups and expired sessions, at no more than 2 requests a second.

02

Collect

Downloads every invoice and delivery proof. Dates come from the page or the PDF; a scan it can't read goes to a person.

03

Reconcile

Checks every line in code against the rate card, the month's fuel surcharge and the delivery proof. The model never does arithmetic.

04

Dispute

Overcharges above ₹500 are queued for a person to approve. The dispute list is checked before submitting and after. Never twice.

05

Report

Returns a result per invoice with evidence, saves a screenshot of every write and closes one trace per task.

What it checks

Eight rules. Every line.

The first three short-circuit: if one fires, the line is worth ₹0. Otherwise weight, rate, surcharge and detention are all checked, and any number can fire.

shipment_unknown

Not our shipment

The shipment isn't in Meridian's records.

SHP-99812 → ₹0 expected
duplicate_billing

Billed twice

Already on an earlier invoice that wasn't cancelled.

10100 then 10200 → later one
no_pod

No delivery proof

The portal has no proof of delivery for it.

POD missing → ₹0 expected
weight_over

Weight over +2%

Billed weight above actual weight + 2%.

1,974.4 vs 1,828.1 kg → +8.0%
rate_wrong

Wrong rate

Rate per kg isn't the rate card's on the booking date.

₹15.70 vs ₹14.20 → rate_wrong
surcharge_wrong

Wrong fuel %

Fuel surcharge isn't the percentage for the booking month.

15% vs 13% → surcharge_wrong
detention_unjustified

Detention not due

Charged, but delivered less than 2 days after the promised date.

1 day late → ₹1,500 not due
math_error

Totals don't add up

Lines plus 12% GST don't match the invoice total.

off by ₹1,180 → math_error
Results

Safe by default. Built to finish.

Never a wrong dispute

A person approves every dispute. The portal's dispute list is checked before and after; a success message alone is never proof. A second run raises nothing new.

Finishes the job

Timeouts, retries with backoff, re-login on expiry and progress saved outside the browser: a failure costs one step, not the whole task.

Evidence for every line

Results carry evidence. Every write gets a masked screenshot, every task one trace, and an evaluation page scores runs against the answer key.
