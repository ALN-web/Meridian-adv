TransIndus Roadways bills Meridian Freight around 600 invoices a month through a portal built in 2009. No API, no export: two people spent three days a month clicking through it.

Our browser agent now does the clicking and the checking. The rules run in code, a person approves every dispute, and every step is traced.

Works the
legacy portal

Logs in, hands one-time passwords to a person, rides out slow pages, error pages, pop-ups and expired sessions, and resumes where it stopped.

Reconciles
every line

Weight within 2%, the rate on the booking date, the month's fuel surcharge, detention against the delivery proof. All checked in code, to within ₹1.

Reads the
delivery proofs

Downloads every POD and reads promised and delivered dates from the page or the PDF. A scan it can't read goes to a person instead of a guess.

Disputes,
only when right

Only overcharges above ₹500, approved by a person, checked against the dispute list before and after submitting. Never a wrong one, never one twice.

Evidence
you can audit

One Langfuse trace per task, masked screenshots of every write, and an evaluation page scored against the answer key.
